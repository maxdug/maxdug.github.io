I am nostalgic for the early days of the internet when I created my first site on Geocities and my first blogs. But... hypocryticaly my brain sends a signal "that's silly" anytime I think about writing a blog again. So... regocnizing this, here I am. I'm not sure my goals, wether this is just for me as some kind of therapy / keeping my mind sharp or if I want to post longer form content that folks around the web might read. 

Partially, I think this is to get me thinking out loud about the weird internet rabbit holes I go down.

Growing up, I was a big big fan of Murder She Wrote and especially the episodes where Jessica Fletcher has an apartment in New York City and teaches a class at the local college. That's the kind of think I see myself doing. And while my language skills are weak.
